Business Impact Software Due Diligence Questionnaire


1.	Business Purpose & Strategic Alignment

•	What core business problem does the software solve?
•	Which departments depend on it?
•	Is it revenue-generating, cost-saving, or support-focused?
•	How does it align with company strategy or objectives?
•	What happens if the software becomes unavailable?
•	Is it mission-critical, important, or optional?
•	Does it create competitive advantage?


2.	Revenue & Financial Impact

•	Does the system directly generate revenue?
•	Percentage of revenue dependent on this system?
•	Impact on customer acquisition or retention?
•	Does it reduce operational costs?
•	What manual work has it replaced?
•	What is the ROI achieved so far?
•	What is total cost of ownership (TCO)?
•	Cost of downtime per hour/day?
•	Financial penalties for outages?


3.	Operational Efficiency

•	Which workflows are automated?
•	How much time is saved per process?
•	Does it reduce errors or rework?
•	Does it improve staff productivity?
•	Number of active users?
•	Can operations continue manually if needed?
•	Are there bottlenecks caused by the system?
•	Training requirements for staff?
 
4.	Customer & Service Impact

•	Does the software directly affect customer experience?
•	Does it improve response times or service quality?
•	Customer satisfaction metrics impacted?
•	History of customer complaints related to the system?
•	Does downtime affect customers directly?
•	Does it provide self-service features?
•	Is performance scalable during peak demand?


5.	Risk & Business Continuity

•	Business impact of 1 hour, 1 day, or 1 week downtime?
•	Is there a Business Continuity Plan (BCP)?
•	Is there a Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)?
•	Are backup systems in place?
•	Does the system introduce regulatory or legal risk?
•	Data breach impact assessment?
•	Vendor dependency risk?
•	Insurance coverage for failures?


6.	Compliance & Regulatory Impact

•	Does the system handle regulated data?

•	Is data residency compliant?
•	Are certifications maintained?


7.	Scalability & Growth Readiness

•	Can it support growth in users or transactions?
•	Can it expand to new regions or branches?
•	Supports multi-language or multi-currency?
 
•	Integration readiness for new tools?
•	Is modernization needed soon?
•	Vendor roadmap aligned with growth plans?


8.	Vendor & Support Dependence

•	Is the system vendor-supported or internal?

•	Vendor lock-in risks?

9.	Change Management & Adoption

10.	Strategic & Competitive Impact
